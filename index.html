Privacy Policy

Well and Truly Stuck

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Well and Truly Stuck operates an eBay shop and uses an automated application to communicate with customers about their purchases.

Information We Process

Our application may process eBay order identifiers, buyer usernames, purchased items and order details needed to send purchase-related messages.

How We Use Information

We use this information to identify completed orders, send purchase-related communications, and prevent duplicate automatic messages. We do not use this information for unrelated marketing.

Data Storage and Security

Our application uses Cloudflare infrastructure. Access to customer information is restricted. Information is retained only as long as necessary for order communications, duplicate-message prevention and applicable legal obligations.

Sharing Information

Information is processed through eBay and Cloudflare. We do not sell customer information to third parties.

Your Rights

Under applicable UK data protection law, you may have rights to access, correct or request deletion of your personal information.

Contact Us

For privacy enquiries, please contact Well and Truly Stuck through our eBay shop messaging system.
